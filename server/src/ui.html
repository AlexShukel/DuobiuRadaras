Each device (identified by its IP) gets its own chart. "+ pothole" / "+ bump" on a panel label that device right now (like the button in the phone app); keys: Q/A for the first device, W/S for the second, E/D for the third (top row = pothole, home row = bump). Hover a chart and press 1 (pothole) or 2 (bump) to place a label exactly at the cursor's moment. Ctrl+Z undoes the last label added from this page (on the hovered device, or anywhere when nothing is hovered); labels sent from a phone are never undone here. "on chart" plus a click marks an earlier moment instead. Both are saved to that device's labels.json like a label from the phone. Polls /api/devices and /api/raw?device=<ip> every second; keeps the last 6 hours per device in the browser and on the server, which writes everything to devices/<ip>/raw.json.
